The short answer

The loop is a farm day, and a day is a turn FC1

No clock runs, and nothing happens while the app is closed:

  1. plant, tend and assign;
  2. end the day: the night moves every job, crop and expedition a day on;
  3. collect and ship;
  4. the bin pays overnight;
  5. spend the gold on seeds, animals, upgrades, land and beds;
  6. the farm grows, Umas rank up, chapters and festivals come round.

Seven activities, all dispatched, none real-time

  • farming, the ranch, mining, fishing and foraging;
  • the workshops: the Kitchen (cooking), the Restaurant (serving) and the Forge (merge);
  • expeditions: parties of three or four Umas sent to a region for a few days, resolved by their numbers, with a story log.

Many things to rank up

  • the farm's Rank;
  • each Uma's rank in each activity, with talents from her own stats;
  • tools and buildings;
  • crop and product quality;
  • animals' affection;
  • bonds between Umas, and your affinity with each Uma.

Something always to aim for

  • a requests board;
  • a Farm Diary of collections and milestones;
  • a festival every season: crop show, cooking contest, animal show, fishing derby, and a race cup;
  • eight light story chapters, each a short goal list.

Relationships grow by working together

Umas who share a place, a party or a festival team build a bond, and each level opens a short conversation between them. You build your own affinity with each Uma by chatting, gifting and praising her work.

The order

The loop's rules and screens on today's map first, so it plays soon; the HD-2D farm when the shared stage exists (FR2); the other games later.

It builds on hd2d-farm.md (FR1–FR24), which you accepted whole (D21): the HD-2D engine, Umas walking as sprites, taps to assign work, and workshops with genre minigames. It changes:

  • plan.md: FM6, FM8, and FM9–FM15 (parcels, now later);
  • spec.md: FS4–FS11 (orders and no currency become selling and gold);
  • FR19: the Restaurant earns gold, and the crew's boost moves to the Kitchen.

2 · The loop: a farm day is a turn FC1FC2FC3FC4

A few minutes of taps, then End the day, and the night settles it (a day's turn). Read it clockwise from Spend. What you collect is shipped or kept; the bin pays overnight, when you end the day; the gold buys the next day's plan. Grow branches off the loop and feeds back into it by opening land, buildings, places, regions and visitors.

    3 · Time: a day is a turn FC5FC6FC33FC34

    Time is turns (D25, your call, 2026-11-08). There's no real time and no in-game clock. A farm day is a turn you end yourself with End the day, and the night settles everything. Nothing moves while the app is closed.

    The calendar moves only when you end the day FC5FC22

    A season is 12 days, so a year is 48: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Each season's festival falls on its 10th day, and the Harvest Cup closes the year on Winter 12.

    ★ the season's festival, on day 10Cup: the Harvest Cup, Winter 12, the year's finaleA festival day is the festival: its event fills the day (FC33)

    Days are what you spend. Festivals fall on fixed dates, requests are due by a date, and crops grow only in their season, so ending a day with Umas idle is a day lost. The calendar, in the account bar, shows the date, the season and the next festival.

    Work is counted in days FC6

    When you end the day, every job moves one day on.

    WorkDaysOn a 10-day scale: solid the shortest, pale up to the longest
    • A queue means "keep at this for N days". Repeat starts the same job the next morning.

    Every outfit on the release can join in the end: the roster can reach all 272, and beds cap who lives and works on the farm at once (60). A bigger crew runs more places and more expeditions, so growing it is part of the progression, as the harvest sprites and Rune Factory's monsters were. This changes FM6 ("every outfit from day one").

    The alternative is FM6 as it stands: all 272 at once, with only slots as the limit. It's easier, but the roster stops being something you build.

    Talents from her stats FC17

    Each Uma has a talent grade, S to G, in each activity, read from her master-data stats by one rule, the same for everyone. It's data, so no names are in git. A talent speeds her work and lifts quality.

    One rule for everyone (D24 note 10): Power → mining and the Forge; Stamina → farming; Guts → the ranch; Wit → cooking and fishing; Speed → foraging and serving; all five → expeditions.

    Her running-style calling (FM7, built in F4) stays as her favourite: she works that line a little happier, bonding faster. It's no longer her only line.

    Six ranks per activity, earned by doing FC18

    Fantasy Life's idea, with the farm's own names, held by each Uma rather than the player.

    Each Uma climbs this ladder in each activity. ⚑ a challenge clears each rank-up.
    • Work raises it: each job adds rank points, as Craft Level does today.
    • A challenge clears it: each rank-up asks for one, for example ship 20 crops of 3 stars; reach Mine floor 10; win a contest entry.
    • It pays: faster work, better quality and deeper places (FC11).
    • Today's single Craft Level becomes her rank in her calling's line, so the save carries over (FC31).

    One festival a season, on its 10th day FC22

    Each season's own, with your entries scored against two rival farms; the Harvest Cup closes the year on Winter 12 (the calendar). A festival day is the festival itself: its event fills the day (FC33). Rivals' scores are set by the season and your Farm Rank, so they stay a match. Prizes are gold, decorations, a trophy for the Diary, and often a recruit. The Harvest Cup is a light nod to who the Umas are; uma-racer stays the racing game. Until M7 the Cup shows as a results card with a simple race bar; from M7 it plays back on the stage (D24 note 3).

    The story, light and present FC24

    • The premise: the trainer takes over an overgrown countryside farm where Umas spend their off-season, and brings it back to life with them.
    • Eight chapters, each a short goal list of 3–6 goals with a scene at its start and end.
    • The town's cast is original: the mayor, the shopkeepers, the rival farmers. They aren't Umas, so their lines can live in git.
    • Umas appear in roles filled at runtime: "your first hand", "your best cook", "the Uma you're closest to". Their names come from master data and never from git.

    The eight chapters, and what each opens FC24

    Settled in D24 (notes 2 and 4). The pills are FC32's milestones: each milestone ships the chapters that open its activities, chapter 1 in M4, chapters 2–5 in M5, chapters 6–8 in M6. A goal that needs a later feature (chapter 4's first festival) waits for it.

    • A chapter opens what's new (an activity, a building);
    • the Farm's Rank opens more of what's open (land, capacity, higher regions);
    • upgrades are bought within what's open (the boat, after chapter 7).
