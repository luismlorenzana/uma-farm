The short answer

Day 1 CS2CS3

Pick 3 Umas from 6 (one free redraw). 500g, one block of 9 plots, 9 free Carrot seeds, the Woods and the Pond.

The calendar CS4CS7

Four seasons of 12 days set seeds, fish, demand (+20%) and a festival on day 10. End the day settles the night in nine fixed steps; the morning opens on a summary.

One price rule CS9

A ten-grade ladder (30g → 300g) × the family's factor; refined ×1.5; dishes and made goods ×1.25 their inputs; +25% a star, not compounded.

Work in days CS17CS18

A gatherer brings home 3–5 items a day at Novice, twice that at Legend. Stations turn 8 a day. Crops take 4–10 days, faster when tended.

Talents and ranks CS27CS29

Talent = her 5★ stat × her growth, cut at 150 / 138 / 126 / 114 / 104 / 96 / 88. Ranks at 40 / 160 / 400 / 900 / 2,000 points, 10 a day of work.

The first year CS52

Medians of 30 runs: 2,871g at day 12, 8,945g at day 24, 56,025g at day 48; about 303,000g earned; 20 Umas, Farm Rank 6, chapters 1–5. Never stingy, never runaway.

End the day: the night in nine steps CS7

  1. The bin pays: price × stars × demand
  2. Crops grow: 1 untended, the tender's rate
  3. Animals: fed, affection moves, products
  4. Jobs: uma-work's clock +1 day
  5. Workshops reset
  6. Ranks, bonds, affinity tick
  7. The calendar turns; withering
  8. Goals: requests, visitors, chapters, Farm Rank
  9. The morning summary

The morning summary CS5

  1. the bin's sales
  2. work done
  3. ranks
  4. hearts and conversations
  5. what's new
  6. today's nudges

The nudge CS6

End the day glows All set when every Uma works, nothing waits to be collected and each open workshop is played. Otherwise a badge counts what's left, and a one-line sheet asks "End anyway / Back" (Menu can turn it off).

Seeded nights CS8

Every draw comes from hash(uid, day, what, n): the same save always gives the same morning, so a sync conflict can't re-roll luck.

3 · Prices CS9CS10

The ladder, to scale (gold at ★1, before the family's factor)

Factors: ores ×1.0 · stones ×0.8 · crystals ×1.6 · woods ×0.8 · mushrooms ×1.0 · herbs ×0.9 · fruits ×1.1 · nuts ×1.0 · sweets ×1.1 · fish ×1.3 · hides ×1.5 · bones ×1.6 · essences ×1.8. Crops have their own prices.

Stars: +25% a star, never compounded

Refined goods sell at ×1.5 their input; dishes and the Forge's goods at ×1.25 their inputs; the season's demand adds +20%. Worked: a ★3 Silver Ingot is 65 × 1.5 → 100g, × 1.5 = 150g.

The bin is the floor: requests pay ×1.5, the Restaurant adds 20–40% tips, festivals pay prizes.

The catalogue: all 370 goods CS11–CS19

Every good's id is uma-art's own, so names come from ids. Four need new art (Egg, Duck Egg, Milk, Goat Milk). Days: crops' untended days, a product's interval, a station job's length; 0 for a workshop play or loot.

Crops CS11CS12

22 from the shop, 5 rare. Four blocks of 9 plots (free, 800g, 3,000g, 8,000g at FR 1, 2, 4, 6). A tender grows her block 1.25 a night at Novice → 1.75 at Legend; Ready at days − 0.5. Seeds back 5–35%; crop levels 1–10 at 20 × L points, +0.1 quality each.

The ranch CS13

Seven animals (800g–8,000g), feed 10–20g a day, a carer per pen of 4. Hearts at 40 … 1,300 points; a Novice C carer gains 17 a day. Product stars = hearts ÷ 2.5: 10 hearts is always ★5.

Depth CS14–CS16

The Mine and the Quarry: 30 floors, main grade ⌈floor ÷ 3⌉, ⌈f ÷ 5⌉ Uma-days to find each stairs. Seven fishing spots; six forest areas. Her rank sets how deep she goes.

5 · Quality CS26

stars = 1 + ⌊Q + u⌋, clamped 1–5, u a seeded draw: expected stars = 1 + Q. Q adds up her rank (0.5 a rank), her talent (−0.75 … +1), care (tended +0.25; hearts ÷ 2.5), crop level (+0.1 a level), inputs (mean ★ − 1), tools (+0.25 a tier) and well fed (+0.25). Stations carry the input's stars.

7 · The roster CS34–CS36

The Dorm: beds to scale, cost and Farm Rank CS34

How visitors come CS35CS36

  • The Gate: every fourth morning while a bed is free; she joins at 2 hearts of affinity (about 4 days).
  • Chapters: one at each chapter's end, for the farm's weakest open activity.
  • Festivals: 1st place, one joins; 2nd, one comes to the Gate.
  • Requests: about 1 in 25 brings a recruit. Expeditions: a Great at a new region.
  • Off-season: recruits past the beds stay away, keeping everything; swaps at the Dorm are free, next morning.

Year 1 in the sim: 21 Umas (3 starters, 8 from the Gate, 5 chapters, 4 festivals, 1 request).

The verdict

  • Not stingy: after day 1 there's something worth buying every 1–4 days, each costing 1–4 days of that season's income.
  • Not runaway: the year spends 84% of what it earns and ends with about 4 days of Winter's income in hand, less than Dorm 8.
  • Year 2's sinks (862,000g before decorations) outrun year 1's income; decorations turn any surplus into Farm Rank.
  • Skill pays, gently: Rough-only ends year 1 at 30,000g, Fine at 56,000g, Masterwork at 91,000g (medians of 30).

12 · Save v3 CS53CS54

The shape

calendar, gold, roster (ranks by activity, affinity, well fed), dorm, visitors, items and bin (counts by ★1–★5), work (uma-work on the day clock), fields, crops, ranch, places, buildings, tools, workshops, expeditions, bonds, board, diary, farm, summary. Talents aren't saved; they're read from master data.

v2 → v3

  1. Running jobs finish; queued inputs return.
  2. Materials refunded per grade: raw 10g, refined 15g, made 15g × its refined count, × the grade (the owner's save holds none).
  3. Craft Level → her calling line's rank.
  4. Every Uma who worked joins the roster; the Dorm fits them, free.
  5. Day 1, 500g, Block A, 9 Carrot seeds, chapter 1.
  6. account.updatedAt never moves.
