The short answer

The cast RM1RM4

Only the authored cast: the 20 SS Umas, one outfit each, keyed by character id. No start picks, no Gate visitors, no random recruits: each arrives by D42's plan (the opening three, chapters 1–7, five festivals, Dorm levels 4–8) with an arrival scene.

The start RM8RM10

Name the farmer (never gendered, never drawn); the opening scene brings the owner's three priority Umas; chapter 1 opens. Save v4 is a wipe, the old save kept as the server's backup.

One track RM11RM14

F1–F10, her confession, R1–R10, the proposal, the wedding, S1–S5. Heart n costs 160 + 40n. A level is its event. No decay; points past a full bar are banked.

The day RM19RM22

One evening a day across all Umas, a morning invitation, one of her events a day; once married, End the day asks where you'll sleep.

Events and perks RM25RM31

Each event has one 10–30 s play, graded and never failed, and choices showing their gain. A perk on every level, templates by her line, capped at +40% on any one job.

The sim RM64

Typical: first event day 3, confession day 38, wedding day 84, all 20 day 158. Romance adds +4% to year 1's income, +8% to year 2's, about +40% once all are married.

3 · The day, with its evening RM19–RM23

Time is still turns (D25). The romance adds one scarce choice a day, whom to spend the evening with, and offers that wait for you. Rose marks what's new.

  1. Morning summarythe bin, work, ranks, then Hearts: Ready lines, the invitation, the house's morning, birthdays

One evening a day RM19

Across all Umas; two on every fourth day from 10 married; a second festival night on festival days. Played any time, from her profile or Tonight. Empty costs nothing.

Invitations RM20

At most one a morning (two from 10 married), in her favourite kind, drawn by seed among those you've seen least. Yes: +20 and ×1.5. Another day costs nothing.

Events wait RM21

One of hers a day; Ready events wait forever and never fire on their own. A typical player plays about 4 a day between days 40 and 120.

4 · Heart events and their plays RM24–RM30

Ready when, all four RM24

  1. her bar is full (or it's the day after, for the confession, proposal and wedding);
  2. she's home;
  3. none of hers has played today;
  4. its one setup is met: she works a place today, a good is in the Warehouse, a bond's level, a chapter.

Never an hour, the weather, a season or a festival. What's missing is said on her profile and in the morning.

A heart event, 60–120 s RM29

A place card · 3–5 beats in bubbles of 3 lines · one 10–30 s play · 1–2 choices of 2–3 options, each showing its gain (+30 best, +10 the others, a taste, an item's cost) · the level-up card with its perk and Memory. Confession, proposal and wedding run 3–5 minutes.

6 · The ring bench RM38–RM40

Rings before M6's Forge

The morning after your first Romance 8, Ida Stone opens the Forge's building as a ring bench, with no game: a ring of grade g from 3 refined goods of grade g, any family; CS25's 2 ingots + 1 cut gem from chapter 3. No gold, one a day. The rings are CS25's ten, already drawn.

3 Cedar Planks (G3, 180g)→Iron Ring (340g)
3 Salmon Fillets (G5)→Cobalt Ring

Her ring's bonus, once married RM39

+1% a grade at her line, plus the merge play's +0/+1/+3/+5 from M6: up to +15%. Give her a better ring any day; she keeps the best. No house, bed, gold or calendar gate.

7 · Married life: the homestead RM41–RM47

The Farmhouse and six houses on a new row. A house has 2 rooms, 4 with its extension. A wedding never waits for a room: without one she keeps her Dorm bed. Each night you pick a house; its morning is hers.

  • each resident +30
  • breakfast (S1): the crew well fed
  • her morning item (S2)
  • a house line
  • unvisited 8 nights: it invites you, warmly; never a penalty

8 · Bonds between Umas, now RM48–RM50

Points and hearts

CS44's: +10 a shared place, +15 an expedition day, +20 a festival team, +5 a shared meal or breakfast, and +10 a night under one roof. Hearts at 50 / 150 / 300 / 500 / 750; ×1.1 side by side at 3; a duo move at 5.

The 31 ties seed them

Strength 3 starts at 2 hearts, 2 at 1, 1 at 0. Her closest friend is her strongest tie; Romance 4 gives that bond ×1.5; in her element (F8) needs a 3-heart partner.

Conversations

Authored for each tie's pair (the pack); CS46's templates for the rest when M6 writes them. New bond hearts show in the morning with Read.

9 · The economy, re-simulated, drawn to scale RM63RM64

The core spec's sim carried to five years (240 days) with the authored roster: 30 runs of each player, medians by day. None is the same farm with no romance. Light: chats with 70%, gifts to 30%, evenings on 70% of days. Typical: chats with all, gifts to 60%, an evening every day. Focused: everything on one Uma until she's married, then the next.

Where year 5's lift comes from (typical)

Each family switched off in turn; they overlap, so the shares add to more than the +38% lift. Romance's costs over 240 days: gifts 331,000g of goods, rings 7,000g, houses 317,000g.

The verdict. The research's pace holds where it gave a number, and all 20 married lands in year 4 (typical) or 5 (light): the long goal stays long. Romance adds +4% to year 1's income and +8% to year 2's, when beds and buildings are bought, and about +40% once everyone is married, when the Farm Rank is 10 and gold buys only houses and decorations. The authored roster, not romance, is year 1's big change: 14 Umas at day 48 instead of CS52's 20, so year 1 earns about 216,000g against 303,000g, never stingy.
