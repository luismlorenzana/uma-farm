The loop

Each line is a row of three places, as in uma-rpg: gather raw items, process them into refined ones, make a made item. The made items are the farm's base items (FM8). The Forge turns them into a chosen game's item (FM9), and the Post sends it.

How a parcel travels

A save is only ever written by its own app; a parcel lives in the farm's parcels; every step is safe to repeat, and an id is never claimed twice (FM10, FM11).
