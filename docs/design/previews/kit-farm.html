The farm kit, kit-farm

F16 · KF1–KF14 · 2026-11-11 (D62) · the contract ChatGPT draws the HD-2D farm's art to, in F9 · kit-farm.md · hd2d-farm.md · the stage request F57

One kit of a new kind, farm, in uma-quest's kit format: 128-texel tiles, a camera at 35° from the south that never turns (only tops and south faces), props as upright cards, full colour lit from the top front-left.

  • Drawn for the farm as it plays today: 23 places on 8 rows and the homestead's 0–3 rows, portrait, 9 tiles across.
  • Buildings are built from modules: three wall sets and four roof sets, plus one or two landmarks a place.
  • Places don't change with work (the Umas show it); only crops grow, drawn by family.
  • A place that isn't open is an overgrown lot that clears when it opens.
  • Two waves: 87 frames for what's open now, 30 for M5 and M6's places.

1. The farm it's drawn for (KF1, KF2)

The layout (KF2)

  • Rows are today's map rows, gather → process → make, left to right. The homestead's rows sit between the yard and the town (F54).
  • A band is 4 tiles deep: a 3 × 3 plot a place and a path row. The farm is 32 to 44 tiles tall, and the camera pans up and down.
  • In a plot, the building stands on the back two tiles. The front row is its apron, where its slots are and the Umas stand.
  • Levels: the ground is level 1. The pond and the quarry pit are level 0, so their banks come from the geometry. The Mine's cliff is a wall at its plot's back.
  • F11 sets the exact tiles, the lanes and the Gate's opening.

The format's openings (KF1, F57)

  • a kind farm with its own required list;
  • a set on building pieces, so a design picks a material;
  • doors two columns wide (256 × 256);
  • mounted pieces placed by the design at a level and column (sails, chimneys, awnings, the mine mouth).

2. Buildings from modules (KF3, KF4)

Each building names a wall set and a roof set. The builder makes its south face one module a level and its roof slope from the set's pieces.

One module is 128 × 128 texels, a level; a door is 128 × 256 (two levels); the wide doors are 256 × 256.

Landmarks tell the places apart

No signs and no words: place names stay uma-kit's chips over the stage (FR7), so a renamed house needs no art.

  • The Mill: sails mounted on its gable
  • The Sawmill: a log pile and a saw bench, open-fronted
  • The Smokehouse: a smoker
  • The Forge: an anvil, a glowing chimney, and the romance's ring bench
  • The Restaurant: an awning and a café table
  • The Gate: an arch three tiles wide, fences either side
  • The Farmhouse: two storeys, a porch and a chimney
  • Every house: one cottage drawing (RM41); a lot not built is four stakes and a rope

3. Work, crops and locked places (KF5–KF7)

Places keep one look (KF5)

An Uma walks in, works at her slot with an emote, waves, and a Ready bubble rises (FR9, FR10). The movement is the work, so a place needs no empty, working and ready art of its own (this revises FR15). Lights glow at night: the furnace, the forge's chimney, the lanterns.

An overgrown lot until it opens (KF7)

It tells the farm's own story, "The Overgrown Farm". It lets wave 1 ship with only the places M4 opens. And it costs four frames, where twelve locked buildings would cost far more.

Crops by family (KF6)

Three shared stages (crop-sprout, crop-growing, crop-withered) and twelve ripe families. The crop's own icon shows in its Ready bubble.

4. Size, style and seasons (KF8–KF10)

Props at the sprites' density (KF8)

A traveler stands about 1.6 tiles (200 texels). A tree is 256 × 384, the bin 128 × 128, and the Gate's arch 384 × 384. The full table, with each prop's size, blocks and light, is in kit-farm.md §4.

Style (KF9)

  • kits.md §5: lit top front-left, painted ambient occlusion, no cast shadows on floors (the game draws a soft shadow under each card);
  • warm and sunny, the Hub farmlands' greens and straw golds;
  • every place unique on its row by its walls, roof and landmark;
  • no text and no Uma art, even on the notice board's papers.

Seasons are light, not tiles (KF10)

Springfresh, soft
Summerbright, warm
Autumnamber grade
Wintercold, pale

The kit is drawn once in a neutral late-spring light, and the stage grades it live. Snowy roofs and bare trees are a later wave, if the light alone doesn't sell winter.

5. Not in this kit (KF11)

  • Umas: sprite sets from uma-platform's sprite pipeline, and standees for outfits without one (FR12, F57).
  • Animals: M5's Coop and Barn bring them with F27.
  • Items and marks: uma-art's items and ui, drawn by uma-kit over the stage.
  • The minigames' scenes: the Kitchen's counter, the Restaurant's floor and the Forge's board are kits of their own (F8, F13, F14).
  • The romance's backdrops: events stay full-screen sheets (RM29) until the stage plays them.

6. What the kit must hold, and delivery (KF12–KF14)

Wave 1, 87 frames F9 wave 1

  • Floors (17): grass ×4, path ×2, tilled ×2, forest floor ×2, water ×2, paving ×2; risers: grass ×2, bank.
  • Buildings (20): the plank and plaster sets (2 walls, 2 windows, a door), the wide plank door, the Shop's door, and two roofs in each of red, thatch, shingle and slate.
  • Props (35): the forest's 6, the scarecrow, dock and reeds, the stations' 5, the yard and town's 9, the homestead's 3, and 9 for overgrown lots and decoration.
  • Crops (15): three stages and twelve ripe families.

Wave 2, 30 frames F9 wave 2

  • Floors and walls (11): the stone set, the Mine's cliff and its top, the quarry pit's floors and riser, the paddock.
  • Props (19): the Mine's, the Quarry's, the furnace, grindstone, anvil, forge chimney and ring bench, the ranch's trough and hay, the Restaurant's awning and table, the Kitchen's herb box, bunting, flagpole, signpost, notice board.

Delivery (KF13)

  1. Draw the wave to the contract, in its style.
  2. Pack it with the export script into atlas.png and atlas.json, and commit the masters and the script (P97).
  3. Make review sheets: the kit on light and dark, a sample band (the Fields, the Mill and an overgrown Coop over a path row), and a scale sheet with the stand-in traveler.
  4. Release a uma-art minor. The owner approves the look. The farm's first packet that draws it pins it.

The names are the farm's API (KF14)

F11's stand-in kit draws every wave-1 name in flat colours, so the art swaps in with no code change, and a missing frame falls back to its stand-in.
