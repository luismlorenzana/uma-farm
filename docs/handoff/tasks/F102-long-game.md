# F102 · Long-game progression

Design packet for Phase 2.7. Read [the roadmap](../../roadmap.md), [the mechanics](../../game-mechanics.md)
and [the story pack](../../story-pack.md). The owner's milestones, schedules and relationship features are
the scope. The proposed settings below give the first playtest something concrete to measure.

| Scale | Progress | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| A day | See the Umas at their town jobs, direct the crew's farm work and choose what to do together at night | Town, Ledger, Tonight |
| A marriage | Move her into the homestead, deepen Greater Love and collect her looks and memories | Homestead and her Uma Library book |
| Five marriages | Celebrate with the latest five spouses | Calendar, morning Ready and Diary |
| Fifty marriages plus one farming year | Gather the fifty for the Greater Festival | Calendar and Diary |
| Year 50 | See a soft ending and keep playing on the same farm | Diary and the existing save |

## Town professions and the seven-day week

Keep the four 12-day seasons and 48-day years. Add a continuous seven-day cycle to the absolute farm day. Day 1
is Monday. The work cycle crosses seasons and years without resetting. Existing occasions use their seasonal
dates, so a wedding anniversary may fall on different weekdays in successive years.

Each profession has an authored weekly day off shared by its two partners. Assign the first three of its other
weekdays to the early-week partner and the last three to the late-week partner. On an assigned town day, one
partner works the counter and the other has her farm shift. The bakery below takes Wednesday off as an example.

| Bakery · Wednesday off | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early-week partner | Town | Town | Free | Town | Farm | Farm | Farm |
| Late-week partner | Farm | Farm | Free | Farm | Town | Town | Town |

- Each launch Uma has an authored profession and fixed partner shift. The 50 launch characters can staff 25
  professions. Before she becomes a farmhand, her three future farm days are personal days in town or at home.
  She keeps her job while dating. After the invitation, three days run her Ledger orders. Marriage changes
  her residence to the farm and keeps her job, farm shifts and free day. If she has never been invited to
  work, keep her personal days and let the player invite her after marriage. In dialogue she addresses the farmer
  as her husband, through a shared relationship-aware token with authored wording where appropriate.
- Model daytime location, farm work, residence and nighttime eligibility independently. A spouse on a town shift
  comes home at night and eats with the household. A town-resident farmhand eats with the crew on her farm
  shifts. A free day has no assigned chores. An authored free-day destination can put her in town as a visitor.
  Town, Calendar, Ledger and Library all show the same schedule. Farm services spending a worker's action
  check for a farm shift and availability.
- **Days off are staggered across professions.** With 25 professions and seven weekdays, aim for three or four
  professions off on each day. A full roster then schedules 21–22 farm workers and 21–22 town workers every day
  before other absences. Give related jobs different days off when assigning the cast. Test daily coverage for
  irrigation, feed, production queues and essential counters. Use existing irrigation, stock buffers and manual
  coverage in the pilot.
- Essential transactions work seven days a week through the same shop, permit and loan screens. If a counter is
  uncovered, use self-service. An expedition or festival can take a scheduled worker out of town. Show the
  absence and cover the counter through self-service. Test overlap between departure, return, shifts and home
  visits. A festival judge who entered with the farm recuses herself and the result uses a neutral card.
- Separate town roles from character identities. A shopkeeper can go through the full romance route even if
  the farmer needs her counter before meeting her. Replace fixed recurring NPC speakers in shops, contests,
  rival cards, the smith scene and town story with the role's available cast member. Use brief fallback copy
  when a role has no speaker. The launch data must validate staffed roles, unique job ownership for each Uma
  and both shifts and one day off per profession. Ship the new scheduling rules with a validated launch-roster
  overlay. Old authored stories can still load alongside it. Keep the last valid overlay if a release has broken staffing.
- **Mayor choice.** The first pass keeps one NPC mayor. If the farmer becomes mayor at the beginning, use
  the same construction, loan and permit services as a town ledger, with the opening, notices and town
  projects adjusted to match. Both paths keep the farm's essential services reachable.

### Proposed profession roster

These are writing positions for 25 shift pairs. They can share existing locations. A position can serve as
an Uma's day job and story setting without adding a minigame or service.

| Places | Primary professions |
|---|---|
| Market and store | Grocer, baker, tailor, seed merchant, courier |
| Café, square and inn | Café host, cook, innkeeper, musician, florist |
| Town services | Smith, carpenter, librarian, archivist, bell keeper |
| Farms and woodland | Rancher, animal nurse, orchard keeper, woodland guide, beekeeper |
| Water and works | Fisher, dock steward, boatwright, miller, expedition outfitter |

The owner assigns the cast to jobs and shifts and sets the staggered day off for each profession. Farm orders
remain the player's choice and use her existing aptitudes and ranks independently of her profession.

## Five-spouse celebrations and the Greater Festival

Queue a celebration the morning after the wedding that reaches 5, 10, 15, and so on through 50. Give each of the
latest five spouses a featured beat, plus a scene involving their group. Earlier spouses can attend. Freeze
the five participants in actual wedding order and keep each celebration Ready until played. Waiting until
an absent participant returns, or marrying more Umas, cannot change the cast of an earlier celebration.

| Marriages | Proposed theme | Proposed first-play reward |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 | Welcome to the homestead | Household keepsake display and first group portrait slot |
| 10 | Supper for ten | Household menu and the existing extra evening and invitation capacity |
| 15 | At the hearth | Shared traditions in the household journal |
| 20 | The Great Harvest | Harvest dance and a memory with the latest five spouses |
| 25 | Market evening | A rotating community order in town |
| 30 | Lanterns at the dock | A harbor commission and a crew memory |
| 35 | A garden walk | A seasonal garden project and display space |
| 40 | A homecoming | A tradition from an earlier group, chosen by the player |
| 45 | Greater Festival preparations | Planning page and a choice of contribution |
| 50 | The fiftieth wedding celebration | Greater Festival date and preparation calendar |

The first wedding still opens the homestead. Keep existing furniture, homes, rewards, wedding dates and
replays. Review the existing +5% yield bonuses at 5 and 15 with the new shift schedule and preserve rewards
already earned. The original 20-spouse Great Harvest remains an earned replay on old farms. Use a separate
ID for the new 20-spouse celebration so it can feature the proper five. Proposal for the annual Festival of
Vows is to preserve any existing recurrence, and start the tradition after the Greater Festival on new farms.

**Housing must scale to 50.** The Farmhouse and six houses currently hold two or four residents each. Expand
the homestead before requiring all spouses to commute from the farm. Give each new spouse an available bedroom
at the wedding. Proposal is to open residence wings ahead of the next five weddings, with optional expansions
and decorations as projects. Preserve old room assignments and furniture, and offer a room to a legacy spouse
waiting in town at the next eligible move-in. Use data-driven residences for later packs.

**The Greater Festival** belongs exactly **48 farm days after the 50th wedding**. A 50th wedding on Spring 4 of
Year 12 gives a Greater Festival on Spring 4 of Year 13. Keep the scheduled date and actual play date separately.
Open optional preparations across the four intervening seasons. The main ceremony becomes Ready on the due day.
If missed, let the player hold a make-up celebration. Keep ordinary festivals and anniversaries available.

All fifty can gather. Stage the event in ten short cohort beats. Let the player spotlight traditions or
memories from earlier celebrations. Preparations can use real farm goods, Restaurant dishes, town work and
expedition supplies. Record the crew that supplied them. An unprepared farm can still play the ceremony.
Preparations change its details and unlock keepsakes. Store one receipt for the festival and each reward.

**Year 50 is independent.** Proposal is to offer the soft ending from Spring 1, Year 50 (absolute day 2,353).
A small household can see it, and a Greater Festival still pending can happen later. Write the closing
chapter from the farm's actual milestones, crew, discoveries and relationships, then continue into Year 51.
After the base ending, future releases can add cast members, professions, scenes and other content to the
same save. Proposal is to offer installed expansion chapters after the Year 50 marker on a farm, with the
activation gate editable per release when the owner decides.

## Married life, Greater Love and True Love

Keep existing relationship points, gifts, heart events and Spouse 1–5. Use **Friendship** and **In Love** as
player-facing labels around the existing premarital ladders. **Greater Love** is a second track per spouse
after marriage. **True Love** is its highest rank. Daytime hearts and gifts do not feed the second track.

### Tonight

1. Open Tonight from the homestead or End day. Allow **five completed visits per night** across solo and duo.
   These have their own slots. A player can end the night early or revisit an eligible spouse within the cap.
2. Choose a spouse home for the night. A town shift ends before Tonight. Check overnight expeditions and
   existing room and birthday preferences. Show who is available and when an away spouse returns.
3. A visit plays the pajama look, sitting conversation, a tender bedside beat and goodbye. Each completed solo
   visit awards **+1 Greater Love**. On visit five, choose **Say goodnight** or **Stay the night**. A confirmed
   stay gives one additional point on waking together, once per night. Ending early can use the current
   overnight routine without a Greater Love bonus. Morning dialogue can be read after settlement.
4. A short fallback still completes the same scene beats. Exiting a visit midway keeps the slot unspent.
   Save the completed visit's day, real participants, scene ID and look as one outcome. Reload, retries
   and replay cannot award points or rotate the look again.
5. Keep visits short enough to work with 50 spouses. Give each speaker personal lines, with authored variation
   and short templates for repeats. Expose the new track and its unlocks in her Uma Library book. Keep F101's
   base completion count and stamp. Give the married-life collection its own progress in the same book.

### Progression and wardrobes

| Collection | Starting state | Proposed Greater Love thresholds | Rotation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ten pajama looks | Look 1 at marriage | 0, 6, 14, 24, 36, 48, 60, 75, 90, 105 | Advance to the next unlocked look after each completed solo visit. Loop in stable order. |
| Five anniversary wedding-night looks | Look 1 from the first anniversary | 0, 24, 48, 72, 96 | Each completed wedding anniversary scene advances its own wardrobe cursor. |
| True Love | Locked | 120 | Award the title once and record the date. |

One visit on 120 nights takes two and a half farm years. Five visits plus a stay with the same spouse takes
20 nights. For all 50 spouses the best possible total is 1,000 nights with six points each. Two visits per night
without the morning bonus takes 3,000 nights, so reaching True Love with every spouse can extend beyond the
soft ending. Run mixed rotations with 1, 5, 20 and 50 spouses before setting the cap.

The anniversary night gets its own occasion, alongside the existing daytime anniversary scene and birthday
priority. It can occupy a Tonight slot on the wedding date. When several spouses have the same anniversary,
each gets her offer. Proposal is to let a missed scene be made up later without granting a second set of
annual rewards. The scene carries the anniversary it represents, with playback date recorded separately.
An anniversary visit uses its wedding-night look in place of pajamas and can award one visit point under the
usual rules. Only its own rotation advances. No duplicate payment, slots or outfit advances when replaying an
earned scene.

Save independent wardrobe cursors. If an unlocked slot has no art or writing, use the existing pajama or
wedding-night portrait and her default lines for that slot. The rotation still advances. The Library can
distinguish reaching a threshold from seeing the full art. Replays use the saved slot and scene context.
The original wedding night continues to use its existing outfit.

### True Love duos

- Add a small owner-facing **Duo editor** to the Farm Story Sandbox. Select two cast IDs, see the partners
  and slots on both sides, add or remove a pair, validate and save. Store one canonical unordered pair
  with the current pair-key convention. Show it under both Umas. Each can have **up to three** partners.
  Refuse self-pairs, duplicate pairs and a fourth partner at either end. Handle sisters' distinct IDs.
- A pair is playable only once **both spouses reach True Love**. The author curates the pairs manually.
  Friendship between the two Umas is a separate system and does not gate a curated pair. The night picker
  shows each pair once and checks the schedule and overnight availability of both people.
- After a duo opens, every eligible night activity offers **Solo** or **Duo**. Start with the visit scene,
  then extend the participant picker to the existing nighttime activities. A duo uses one shared visit
  slot. On the fifth visit the pair can stay if both are eligible. Record both participants and one lead house
  for the existing overnight benefit. Both are at True Love so there is no point overflow or doubled payout.
  Keep ordinary evening activities such as Dinner separate from the five visits. A night activity that already
  has an action or a result card keeps its cost and settlement until explicitly retuned.
- Each spouse's Library links to the same earned duo scene. Removing a pair or retiring a script changes
  future offers and keeps the historical proof. Use the archive if the original source can no longer replay.

## Additional long-term loops

1. **Town Works.** Pick a public build or restoration sponsored by the Umas in the relevant town jobs.
   The farm can supply exact-grade goods from its roster's work. Completion changes a place, opens a service
   or adds a scene. This is the first candidate to prototype with the shift pilot.
2. **Annual commissions.** Choose a seasonal commission from one part of town. Use goods from several farm
   activities or a Restaurant menu. Credit the crew's contributions and turn some commissions into repeatable
   traditions for the Greater Festival.
3. **Farm yearbook.** Pull a few highlights from records the game already holds. Each year show a favorite
   memory chosen by the player, with real weddings, work and discoveries. Reuse the pages in the Year 50 scene.

The 50-Uma [production trial](../../game-mechanics.md#roster-production-playthrough) left ten daytime actions
free on a typical Year 4 day and 26,869 raw goods at year end. Prototype projects that put those two resources
to work. Test new rewards at smaller rosters before pricing them for the largest farm.

## Build order and acceptance

1. **Week and town pilot.** Play two professions with different days off through all four seasons and a year
   boundary. Verify each pair's non-overlapping shifts before and after marriage, self-service on an uncovered
   counter, contested bookings, maintained residence and nighttime availability. Re-run four-season production
   for 10, 20 and 50 Umas and measure daily water and feed, service coverage, meals and stock.
2. **Housing, celebrations and ending.** Verify room availability from the first through fiftieth wedding.
   Test same-day wedding order, ten five-spouse cohorts, pending events, the Greater Festival at +48 days and
   a make-up, then Year 50 into Year 51 on a small and a large farm. Load a test expansion.
3. **Greater Love.** Visit through the first wedding to True Love. Verify slots one through five, early ending,
   fifth-slot stay, next-morning payment, reloads and the sixth-visit refusal. Test every wardrobe slot, bare
   art releases, anniversaries with coincident dates and an extended rotation with 50 spouses.
4. **Duo and Library.** Test the editor and the three-pair cap at both ends. Check earned and archived replays,
   genuine participants and no extra overnight payout. Play both solo and duo at 844×390 and 667×375.
5. **Existing saves.** Add bounded v5 receipts for wedding order, events, milestones, Greater Love, wardrobe
   cursors and scene context. Start Greater Love at zero on old saves. Recover actual wedding dates from
   existing records. Break legacy same-day ties by roster join then stable ID where commit order was lost.
   Keep historical attendance and rewards only when proved. Verify existing housing, the old finale, Diary
   and Library completion, multi-device round-trip, offline reopen and save size across fifty years.

**Owner review.** Confirm the mayor, the 120-point starting cap, which professions to assign first, the proposed
celebration themes and rewards, the Festival of Vows transition, and when expansion chapters become available.
