# F90 — Food: everyone eats, from day one

**uma-farm · Claude · M · Needs: F89 (the field's plots feed the pantry) · [Design](../../design/game-design.md#the-farm-at-scale-2026-11-15)**

> Everyone who eats on the farm eats twice a day. What isn't cooked is bought in as takeout, at a price you feel from
> the first night. Cooking turns the Warehouse's goods into meals in batches, and an Uma on the Kitchen's task feeds many.

## Why

The owner (2026-11-15): "I didn't see any upkeep day 1 … Main character spends daily money to buy food, let's say
takeout, once we have materials stored, he can spend actions to cook, maybe we tie to cooking minigame. The more
characters live in the farm the more dishes need to be purchased or cooked. Umas allocated to cooking and the kitchen
can cook in batches so 1 Uma can support multiple other Umas. (maybe Cooking is an additional skill to Umas)."

## Decided

1. **Who eats:** the farmer, everyone living on the farm, and every farmhand who works that day. Two meals each a day.
2. **The pantry:** cooked meals wait in the pantry (they keep two days). At night each mouth eats from it; whatever's
   short is **takeout**, bought in at 15g a meal and charged with the upkeep. So a new farm pays 30g a night from the
   first night; the morning and the Book show it beside the upkeep.
3. **Cooking:**
   - **the farmer:** "Cook a batch" in the farmhouse kitchen, 1 action: four meals from two goods of the Warehouse
     (crops, fish, eggs, milk, mushrooms; any two). Better goods make better meals (their stars), and a meal of ★2+
     leaves everyone who eats it well fed;
   - **farmhands:** a new task, **Cook**, on the work board: each action cooks a batch, its size rising with her new
     **Cooking** aptitude (4 meals at level 1, up to 12 at level 10), so one cook feeds many;
   - F79's household meals stay: they're the farmer cooking for the household, and they now fill the pantry too.
4. **The cooking minigame** comes with the Kitchen (Phase 2.3): there, a batch the farmer cooks plays the game, and its
   grade lifts the meals. Until then, cooking is the action.
5. **The numbers** are checked by the season simulations (alone, two farmhands, and F91's 10, 20 and 50): takeout must
   bite but never sink a new farm, and cooking must pay for itself once the field yields.

## Do

1. The domain: mouths, the pantry, takeout at night, the farmer's batch, the Cook task and its aptitude, with tests and
   the simulations' numbers.
2. The web: Cook a batch, the pantry on the Book, the morning's food line, the work board's Cook.
3. [The game mechanics](../../game-mechanics.md).

## Done when

- A new farm's first morning says "Takeout · 30g"; cooking a batch the next day feeds the farmer from the pantry.
- With two farmhands, one on Cook, the farm eats from the pantry and pays no takeout.
