# F110 addendum — Heirloom jewelry

**Status · Authoring draft.** Add this to the Forge after the core tools, and validate costs with real farm runs.
Heirlooms give an established farm a reason to keep its mining, refining and planning crews busy and give each
spouse a collection of gifts tied to her formal looks.

## Four keepsakes, five looks

The [shared birthday and anniversary wardrobe](../../game-mechanics.md#greater-love-and-true-love) has five looks
at 0, 24, 48, 72 and 96 Greater Love. Keep the baseline available when she marries. Each of the next four looks
requires its existing Greater Love threshold **and** the matching heirloom gifted to that spouse.

| Formal look | Greater Love | Keepsake slot | Commission | Earliest Farm Rank |
|---:|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | 0 | — | Baseline from marriage | — |
| 2 | 24 | Earrings | Diamond Earrings | 8 |
| 3 | 48 | Necklace | Starstone Necklace | 8 |
| 4 | 72 | Bracelet | Orichalcum Bracelet | 10 |
| 5 | 96 | Brooch | Philosopher’s Brooch | 10 |

Each spouse has four permanent keepsake slots. An order names its recipient and is personalized to her, so a
piece fills exactly one slot in one book. It takes an explicit gift to fill the slot. You can commission a piece
ahead of the Greater Love threshold or work toward any of her four keepsakes independently. Neither her wardrobe
nor anyone else's requires a spouse-count milestone. The fiftieth wedding and Greater Festival keep their timing.

Birthday and anniversary visits still open on their current dates and use the same visit allowance. A spouse
without heirlooms can celebrate in her unlocked formal looks and keep gaining Greater Love. Pajamas, the
120-point True Love milestone, duo eligibility, wedding rings and proposal rules keep their own progression.
A duo host uses her unlocked formal look, and the visiting spouse uses her own pajamas.

## Commissions

The Forge's workshop gets a **Heirlooms** tab alongside Tools. The tab opens at Rank 8 when the restored Forge,
Mine, Quarry, Smelter and Cutter are operating. Browse exact inputs and suppliers before they are unlocked in
**What Next?**. Commission for any current spouse when the recipe's rank and stock checks pass.

| Commission | Exact refined materials, any quality stars | Fee | Ready |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Diamond Earrings | 16 Titanium Ingots (G8), 12 Cut Diamonds (G8), 8 Gold Ingots (G6) | 24,000g | 2 dawns |
| Starstone Necklace | 24 Mythril Ingots (G9), 16 Cut Starstones (G9), 12 Cut Diamonds (G8) | 40,000g | 3 dawns |
| Orichalcum Bracelet | 32 Orichalcum Ingots (G10), 20 Cut Philosopher’s Stones (G10), 12 Mythril Ingots (G9), 12 Runestone Blocks (G9) | 64,000g | 4 dawns |
| Philosopher’s Brooch | 44 Orichalcum Ingots (G10), 28 Cut Philosopher’s Stones (G10), 20 Cut Starstones (G9), 16 Meteorite Blocks (G10) | 96,000g | 5 dawns |

These are exact item IDs and grades. The quantity of each ingredient is fixed, and the order accepts mixed stars
of the same required item, with the lowest available stars selected first. Grade and volume create the challenge
through the existing material chains. A solo player who reaches the floors can source every ingredient with the
existing quality rules. A staffed farm can assign gathering and refining to Umas.

One full set costs **224,000g and 272 refined materials** at these prices. The full launch collection is 200
pieces for 50 spouses, totaling **11.2 million gold and 13,600 refined materials**. A single set should feel like
a major project for one favorite spouse. Completing the whole Library's collection is an optional multi-year goal.
The totals are tuning inputs, with particular attention to the Quarry's lower gem frequency and the volume of
refining it creates.

### Exact-grade supply at endgame

The live digs work their deepest reached floor. At floor 30, for example, the Quarry can supply G9 and G10 but
has left the G8 Diamond band behind. Exact sourcing needs a small extension to the existing mine task before
either tool or jewelry recipes rely on those late-save material paths.

- Let the player work a reached floor and target the same floor in an Uma's Mine or Quarry order. The default
  remains her existing deepest-floor assignment. Floor selection reuses the normal building and reach checks.
- Link shortages to representative reached floors by grade. Gold comes from floors 16–18, Titanium and Diamonds
  from 22–24, Mythril, Runestone and Starstone from 25–27, and Orichalcum, Meteorite and Philosopher's Stone
  from 28–30. Use the live dig tables for grades and availability, including the normal neighboring-grade draws.
- Earlier floors share their dig's daily thinning and tool-charge tally. Only work on the deepest floor contributes
  to its stairs. Reports and skill credit read the actual floor and actor. A floor visit cannot reset the cap.
- Offer **Track materials** on a commission. Show free exact stock, protected stock, loads queued, and what is
  missing. Link to or propose exact Smelter and Cutter targets through existing station-plan controls. The player
  confirms any changes to their orders. Use the current stock protections, covering food, Restaurant and other
  plans. A pending selection spends nothing. At confirmation, recheck the previewed lots and follow the normal
  opt-in override if the player explicitly chooses to use reserved lots.

### Order, delivery and gift

1. **Choose a spouse and a piece.** Show her Library look, Greater Love, the art release state, materials,
   fee and ready date. Use distinct states for **Needs materials**, **Artwork coming**, **In progress**, **Ready
   to give**, **Gifted · Needs Greater Love**, and **Look unlocked**. An absent artist asset is a content status.
2. **Commission** from Morning or Afternoon for one player action, the listed gold and the exact goods. There
   is one jewelry order slot at Rank 8 and two at Rank 10, independent of the active tool order and daily Ring
   Bench limit. Each recipient and slot allows one pending, ready or gifted piece. The due date is set at
   commissioning, including when the recipient or assistant is away. A booked helper can get credit using the same one
   Morning Forge assistant action and Forging path as tools. Map Rank 8 recipes to Gold credit and Rank 10
   recipes to Mythril credit, with one credit per assisted commission.
3. **Collect the finished gift from her Library or the Heirlooms tab.** At dawn the order moves to **Ready to
   give**, frees its work slot, and appears in the Daily Report. **Give to {Uma}** places it in her keepsakes.
   Giving is a free acknowledgement available outside her birthday and anniversary. It consumes no evening slot,
   and adds no Greater Love or farm stat bonus. The piece is a personalized keepsake, kept with her book and
   outside the usual shipping, recipe and stock systems.
4. **Wear the look.** Earned looks join the existing formal rotation. The next available slot is the next
   eligible numbered slot after the saved cursor, wrapping to the first eligible slot. If looks 1, 3 and 5 are
   available, rotate 1 → 3 → 5 → 1. Offer **Choose look** for the host of a real birthday or anniversary visit
   so a newly earned outfit can be used on her next occasion. The choice must be eligible at commitment. Record
   the actual slot and continue rotation from it. Replays use the original saved outfit and participants.

## Library, scenes and art

- **Married Life.** Show four keepsake silhouettes beside the five existing portrait slots. Each opens its
  progress and **Plan in Forge** or **Give to {Uma}** as appropriate. Readiness, ownership and seen-in-a-scene
  are distinct. A first visit in the look records the ordinary scene receipt. Its keepsake records the gift date
  and any credited maker separately.
- **Calendar and Night visits.** Show an optional note such as “Birthday night · Starstone look available.” A
  missing piece or pending delivery never removes a celebration or delays the baseline scene.
- **Art.** Four original item icons for the whole game. Use the existing `wedding-anniversary-1` through
  `wedding-anniversary-5` slots and the approved neutral, tender and loving expressions. Each jewel grants
  access to one complete authored look that includes its accessory. The same formal look can appear in both
  contexts, with their existing birthday and anniversary dialogue. An engraving or short line may be written
  for her in the private story source. The default is a simple gift acknowledgement.
- **Release check.** Enable paid commissioning for a mapped look only when its distinct numbered portrait is
  published and the birthday and anniversary preview both resolve that look. The catalogue can still show the
  planned item and recipe. Default dialogue is valid. An art outage after ordering must keep the gift, look
  entitlement and replays safe, using existing portrait fallbacks until it resolves. The status prevents spending
  on unpublished looks without requiring new assets for legacy outfit memories.

## Save and rotation contract

Use one pure helper to return eligible formal slot IDs, shared by the Library, Calendar and new-visit planner.
Extend the old points-only wardrobe logic to read permanent gift and grandfathered slot records. Keep the
numbered art keys, five slots, visit limits and original memory key. The replay path reads the recorded visit.
For a deliberate look selection, validate the requested slot alongside the existing host, visit and day checks.

On first upgrade of an existing save, capture every formal slot already open under the old points-only rule
per spouse, and any original formal slot in her recorded memories. Store the grant once, so later point gains
use the new requirements. Earlier unlocks and their scenes are kept. The four keepsake slots are still available
to collect for existing spouses, with their own receipts. A new save and a spouse married after migration use
the new wardrobe rule from the first look onwards.

Keep at most two active orders and one compact record per recipient and keepsake slot. Validate stable recipient
and recipe IDs, ranks, per-slot uniqueness, eligible dates and statuses. Start, settle and gift each order once.
Recheck the current save after reload or sync before consuming an action, withdrawing lots, advancing an order
or presenting a piece. Recognize migrations in the parser and preserve legacy memories and cursors. Old client
and outage handling follow the base F110 save contract. The player can progress the project even if the recipient
is in town or on an expedition.

## Balance and acceptance

- Run fresh and legacy solo fixtures. Confirm her first wedding, six-log Copper Ring, first birthday and
  anniversary, Greater Love, True Love and duos with empty jewelry slots. Play a present-day legacy outfit and
  replay a previously seen one after migration.
- Run matched 10-, 20- and 50-Uma farms from Rank 8 through Rank 10 and at least a 120-day endgame year. Start a
  first set from normal warehouse stock. Track days to first piece and full set, cost against discretionary cash,
  Mining and refining actions, permanent material shortfalls, meals, upkeep and exact Restaurant supplies.
  Target one complete favorite-spouse set in roughly one to two seasons after access to all floors on a focused,
  established crew-run save. Test the full launch collection's projected duration and ensure slot throughput
  can accommodate the materials supplied. Tune fees, volume and lead times from those results.
- Reach floor 30 first and source all recipes with ordinary manual and crew play, including G6 Gold and G8
  Diamonds. Confirm shared thinning, caps, no duplicate stair progress, material grades and named work credit.
- Complete all four recipes. Test sparse looks and manual selection, gift before and after the Greater Love
  threshold, both occasions on the same date, duo host and guest looks, missing art before purchase, and
  temporary missing art after purchase. Review both landscape sizes.
- Run a 50-spouse fixture through all 200 recipient-slot states. Verify the same piece can be commissioned for
  another spouse, a gift cannot be duplicated, two active slots persist through reload, and tool orders and the
  daily Bench craft work in parallel. Repeat the soft ending milestones with empty slots.

The Forge workshop, exact-floor sourcing and keepsake records can be implemented in Farm. Character portraits,
expressions and any individualized gift lines follow the established private art and story release workflow.
Roll out recipes for mapped looks as the art arrives. The two-counter decision in the [base packet](F110-forge-rework.md#where-rings-could-live)
still governs how the Ring Bench is navigated.
