# F106 · Day parts and morning crew work

## Starting point

The live farm has ten general actions. Talks, gifts and Ready scenes cost one each. A date costs two, uses a daily
outing slot and has its own progression gates. The farm normally has one outing slot, with later household and
festival exceptions. A festival grants three bonus event actions first and can fall back to regular actions.
Married life already has five shared night visit slots. The last visit can carry a stay that adds one point at dawn.

Prototype five daytime actions marked Morning, five marked Afternoon and the existing five Night visits.
Fifteen general actions would raise the pool for talks and gifts by 50% and accelerate farm work on a solo save.
Keep the night pool for spouse visits. Leave dates’ slot limits and existing rewards in the first trial. At six
points per night at most, True Love’s 120 points still take at least 20 nights of focused visits.

| Part of the day | First trial | Typical actions |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | First five regular actions. Review the crew, then resolve its work as the farm enters Afternoon. | Farm chores, staff orders, talks and gifts |
| Afternoon | Last five regular actions. Ingredients and goods from the completed shift are in the Warehouse. | Dates, errands, festivals, finishing the farm |
| Night | Five existing shared married-life slots. The shared fifth-visit stay carries forward to dawn. | Casual, birthday and anniversary visits |

## Put the crew shift before afternoon

- Reuse the farmhand engine and town rota. Apply available stock, grade floors, exact-grade recipes,
  ingredient reserves, aptitudes, worker reservations, half pace and expedition presence.
- Show a shift forecast in the morning. On crossing five regular actions, resolve the shift once and store dated
  receipts. Prototype **Start afternoon** for players who skip the rest of the morning, showing how many actions
  will pass. Free menus, travel and bonus-only festival scenes do not advance the regular clock. A two- or
  three-action move can span the boundary and still resolve the shift just once.
- Reuse the stored shift for contest, aptitude, production and bond credit. Keep crop growth at night. Track
  afternoon farmer work separately. The crew’s harvest is available for afternoon recipes and shipping.
- Show whose orders run now and whose changes apply on her next farm shift. Contests, Restaurant and Kitchen
  read remaining worker capacity. Check that they cannot charge actions already used or credit the wrong producer.
- An older save already partway through a day finishes that date under the previous timing. Reload, offline sync,
  and ending the day early must resolve each scheduled shift exactly once.

## Compare with the deployed rhythm

Play a fresh solo farm and 10-, 20- and 50-Uma farms through one 120-day year. Compare the two timings with the same
rota, seed, opening gold and upgrade path. Record actions spent by phase, seeds and exact grades available by
afternoon, spare crew capacity, harvests lost, shipping gold, takeout and arrears, days to Friendship 3, first
date, confession, and each later relationship milestone reached. Use an existing married fixture for the
20-night focused True Love check and the household cap.

Cover a tight shipping day, a festival, an expedition return, a date crossing the fifth action and a last-visit
stay. Check both landscape sizes and mornings with zero orders or too few workers. Keep early talks, dates,
contest entry, first-year cash flow and all five nightly visits reachable without requiring the player to burn actions.
