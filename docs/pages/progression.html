Progression without global chapters

Adopted by the owner, 2026-11-13 ("this progression and update makes sense to me"). The farm has no global chapters and no global story. The farm grows by what you earn and grow, and by the lives you build with the Umas. The story lives in each Uma, in her own chapters. It's built by F64 and F68, and moves into the game mechanics as it lands. The page view is progression.html.

The farm's unlocks: three kinds

1. The Farm Rank says what you may build

The rank comes from income and resources only: gold shipped (1 point per 100g), a Diary entry for each good first shipped, and the Diary's milestones. A draft of what each rank offers; the numbers get tuned by a simulation of a whole run:

2The CoopBlock B, new seeds
3The Barn, the Sawmill, the SmokehouseThe Dorm's next levels
4The Mine, the SmelterBlock C, new seeds
5The Quarry, the Cutter, the Kitchen
6The dock, then the boat (the sea's spots)Block D
7The expedition post, the Restaurant
8The Forge's merge game
9–10—The farm's gold crest at 10

2. You build it with gold and farmed goods

  • A cost each: every place has its cost in gold and the farm's own goods. For example, the Coop might be 3,000g + 40 planks, and the Mine 6,000g + 60 planks + 20 stone blocks; the numbers get tuned.
  • Built overnight: a place you pay for stands the next morning.
  • What's next: the farm's projects list shows what you can build and what's still missing.
  • The same rule as today: the Sawmill and the Smokehouse already work this way (Farm Rank 3, then gold). This makes it the rule for every place.

Her chapters

  • Her story is hers: her own chapters, from the day you meet her to married life.
  • The story decides them:
    • how many there are, their titles, and which of her events each holds;
    • nothing is required (the open pack's menu);
    • a chapter is the story's grouping of her events along the game's backbone.
  • The game's backbone stays as built:
    • friendship F1–F10, then the confession;
    • romance R1–R10, then the proposal and the wedding;
    • married life S1–S5;
    • the points, the perks and the evenings along the way.
  • Default chapters, when the story gives none:
    1. "Getting to know her": friendship, to the confession;
    2. "Together": romance, to the proposal;
    3. "The wedding";
    4. "Married life".
  • A chapter may ask the farm for something: the story's choice, and optional. For example, "her third chapter begins once the Kitchen is built", or "once you've shipped 50 of her favourite good". This ties her story to the farm without a global plot. The gates come from the same menu as her trigger to meet.
  • Where you see it:
    • her profile shows her chapters, the one she's in, and what opens the next;
    • the Hearts tab shows each Uma's chapter at a glance;
    • the morning says when a chapter opens ("A new chapter with her").
  • Her ending: her last chapter closes in married life. The household's scenes go on after it.

The long game

There's no global ending. Instead: - the farm: every place built, every block, the Dorm's last level, Farm Rank 10's gold crest; - the people: as many stories as you choose to follow, each to its own end, and a homestead full of houses; - the years: festivals, seasons, records, and the household's milestones.
