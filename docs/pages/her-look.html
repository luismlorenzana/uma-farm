Her look: outfits and expressions

A plan, for the owner's go (2026-11-13). Each Uma gets a wardrobe (four seasonal outfits, spring her default, a kimono, a dinner dress, a wedding dress and pajamas) and busts that change expression in dialogue. ChatGPT draws a bust for each outfit and a face for each expression; the game puts the face over the bust. The page view is her-look.html.

Busts that change expression

A bust per outfit, a face per expression

  • The bust: her head and shoulders in one outfit, with her calm neutral face, on a transparent background. One for each outfit.
  • The face: a square picture of just her face (brow to chin, cheek to cheek, with her bangs) in one expression, drawn to sit exactly over the face of her bust. One for each expression.
  • Why one set of faces fits every outfit: her head is the same in every bust (same size, spot, angle and bangs). Only what's below the neck, and what she wears on her head, changes with the outfit.
  • The format is settled later: sizes, and how a face sits on a bust, are worked out once the pilot's pictures are in Uma Character Art. Until then the pictures go up as a universe-looks collection, dialogue-busts, with pnpm art:push (uma-platform P163): nothing new is built there yet, and its earlier draft of a bust family is parked until the format is settled.
  • The game composes them: the bust, with the face over it, swapped when the expression changes. Both are cached on the device.
  • Fallbacks:
    • a face not drawn yet shows its fallback (the table below), ending at the bust's own neutral face;
    • a bust not drawn yet for that outfit shows her spring bust;
    • an Uma with none shows her single master-data bust, as today.
