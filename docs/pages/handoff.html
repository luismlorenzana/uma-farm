Open work

What's being built right now, and what's waiting on you. Where the game is going is in the roadmap; how it works today is in the game mechanics. The page view is handoff.html.

Now

The rework (the owner, 2026-11-14): the game turns landscape, with a day of actions, a farm loop in the classic farm-life spirit, farmhands who take the work over, a navigable town, the dialogue engine, upkeep and loans, a visible calendar, and a default Uma. The design is game-design.md, and the steps are in the roadmap. Built one at a time, each merged and deployed before the next. Landscape (F74) is in: the app plays sideways, with the bar, the rail and side panels, and the portrait HD-2D preview is retired. The dialogue engine (F75) is in: every scene plays on one engine, the pilot's two Umas show their portraits from master data, their faces change with each line's emote and their outfits follow the scene (the dialogue engine). The default Uma (F76) is in: every Uma without portraits plays on the blank bust, a silhouette in the outfit's colours with a face for each of the 22 expressions, and every Uma without her story plays the complete default story, from meeting her through the wedding night to married life, each piece replaced the moment hers lands (what plays without the story). A day of actions (F77) is in: ten actions a day, the tile farm worked by hand with daily watering, one-action foraging, chopping and fishing, a station's one load, a crate shipped for one action, and going into town for the shop; save v5, so a farm from before it starts over. The calendar (F82) is in: the bar's date opens the year on one screen, each day with its festival, New Year's, the story's holidays and minor days, birthdays, anniversaries and the faces of the Umas with an event that day; her event costs an action, a special day's event actions first, so several Umas' events fit a festival; and the occasion scenes play on their days (New Year's from the second year, the Flower Show, the Fishing Derby's night) (the calendar). The town map (F78) is in: a map you walk around in landscape, who's at each place now (the story's home, job and routine, else the day's draw), the town's people at their posts, and her menu at the design's costs: introduce yourself, talk and give a gift, an action each, her evening as built, her event when it's Ready; no welcome week (the town). Upkeep and loans (F81) is in: each night, after the bin pays, the loans take their share of the night's income and the upkeep is charged (15g a place or house built, 5g a Dorm bed past the first three, 10g an Uma living on the farm; a new farm owes nothing); short of it, the rest is owed, the morning warns, and from the second short night the farmhands work at half pace until it's paid. The mayor's office in town lends what a build, a Dorm level or a house is short of gold, ordered in the same tap (a 10% fee, a tenth a night from income, up to 1,000g at Farm Rank 1, two loans from Farm Rank 4). Ordering a build with your own gold stays on its card, free. The Book shows the upkeep beside the last seven nights' income; the projects list shows each loan and when it clears (upkeep and loans). A simulated first year pays its way: the Mill early on a loan, never a short night, a fifth of the income to upkeep. The Uma loop (F79) is in: her stage from acquaintance to married on her profile and her menu (a friend at Friendship 3, a farmhand once you invite her to work, an action), hangouts and dates at two actions with her scene after the play, Dinner in town (Romance 3) and A night in (Romance 6), moving in after the wedding night, and married life's meals (breakfast or dinner, an action each: everyone well fed and +10) and the night's companion, chosen at Tonight, who greets you in the morning in pajamas. Talks, gifts and the invitation to work need her in front of you: on the farm, or in town where she is today; her Ready event plays from anywhere (the Uma loop). Illustrations (F84) are in: at her big moments the scene opens onto her illustration, full screen in landscape, the text box over its lower edge and no bust while it's up: meeting her, a summer date at the shore, her confession (the question, then her yes), the wedding, the wedding night as it begins and as it fades, a night in, and the morning after the night's companion; the story can name one with an illustration step. Each one seen joins her gallery for good, which her profile opens; the rest show locked with their titles. The first Uma's nine are master data's (uma-data 1.8.0); an Uma without any plays as before (her illustrations). Farmhands (F80) are in: a friend you invite to work does her part-time job in town, then gives the farm 3 actions a day (+1 settled in a season on, +2 once she's your spouse, +1 the day after a household meal; half at half pace) on the tasks you set on the Book's work board: harvest, water, plant, clear and till over the field's rows, a station's load, a forest area's forage or chop, a fishing spot's catch. She works them overnight before the crops grow, and the morning reports her work. Each task has an aptitude from 1 to 10, started by her talent and raised by each action, with more goods, better stars and tricks (two tiles an action, a row watered in one, a bigger haul, two casts, a bigger load); her profile's Work side shows them. Her work pays her rank points, bonds, her line and the perks' extras as an Uma's work does, and she costs 10g of upkeep a night wherever she lives. A simulated year with two farmhands pays its way many times over: 3.4 times the farmer alone's shipments, Farm Rank 3 by Summer's end (farmhands). The ranch and the mine (F83) are in, and with them every step of the rework: the Coop (Farm Rank 2) and the Barn (Farm Rank 3) hold up to eight animals each, bought at the rancher's in town (free there): chickens and ducks for eggs, cows and goats for milk, sheep, alpacas and cashmere goats for wool and fleece. Each day an animal wants feeding (an action and a feed from the silo) and care (an action, +8 fondness); a fed one leaves its good by morning, one left hungry loses fondness and never dies; Collect takes a building's goods for an action, starred by each animal's hearts (ten is always ★5). The silo stands with the Coop or the Barn: grass cut on a weeds tile (an action for 5, the weeds grow back) or fodder at 10g. The Mine (Farm Rank 4) and the Quarry (Farm Rank 5) go thirty floors deep: a face is an action for two finds (ores; stones and gems), a grade every three floors, and its faces find the floor's stairs, taken free, as deep as the Farm Rank reaches; the deepest floor is kept. The Smelter and the Cutter take loads like the other stations, and the Mill weaves wool into cloth. On the work board, a farmhand cares for the Coop or the Barn (feed, collect, care) or works a dig's face, with the Animal care and Mining aptitudes and their tricks. The catalogue grows to 284 goods: uma-art's fibers, and the eggs and milk, drawn in code until uma-art's atlas has them. A simulated two years with the Coop and the Mine never runs short, and each pays more than its nights cost (the ranch, the Mine and the Quarry).

Across the repos

  • uma-platform holds the shared pieces: master data, uma-kit, uma-work, uma-server, uma-art, and the HD-2D stage @luismlorenzana/uma-stage, which the farm no longer uses (its portrait preview retired with landscape). Shared changes go there as requests (change protocol).
    • uma-kit's landscape pieces shipped in uma-kit 0.20.0, and the farm draws its frame with them: the left rail, the thin top bar, the wide side panels, Account's two columns and the update prompt clear of the sides. The place map that runs across (HubMap flow="across") is there for a map that needs it; the farm's own tile map doesn't.
  • The art split: ChatGPT draws character art (Uma sprites, poses, busts, portraits). Claude draws everything else in code (items, UI marks, buildings, the HD-2D kits) in a uma-platform session.
  • uma-quest shares the story's way of working (the Uma Ranker's cast, tags and ties), read as local files on the owner's PC.
  • uma-rpg is frozen. The farm's v2 save engine was copied from it, never imported.
  • Parcels to uma-quest, uma-rpg and uma-racer are parked until the farm's own game is done. uma-quest's accept list is in its spec at a88838b (its clean slate removed it from main).

How a packet reads

A packet is one piece of work, written from the roadmap when it starts: - Why, and what to read first; - Decided: the calls already made, so the worker doesn't re-decide them; - Do: the steps; - Done when: the checks, including the owner's phone look when there is one; - Deliver: the branch, the pull request, the game mechanics updated for any rule change, and the packet deleted.
