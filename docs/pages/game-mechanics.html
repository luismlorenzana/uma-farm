Game mechanics

How uma-farm works today, as the code runs it. This is the one description of the game: every rule change updates it in the same commit, and where it and the code ever differ, the code wins and this document is fixed. The page view is game-mechanics.html; where the game is going is in the roadmap.

What's live and what isn't. The deployed game is one game on save v5: a day of ten actions on a tile farm the farmer works by hand, its places, the farm growing by building, and the romance (Part 3), played from the story pack, and played whole without one: wherever the pack is silent, the default story plays, and every Uma without portraits plays on the blank bust (see What plays without the story). The game is laid out sideways, for a phone held in landscape (see the frame). A save from before v5 starts over (see Save versions).

Part 1 — The farm's days and work

A farm day is a turn of ten actions. The farmer works the farm alone, by hand: each tile cleared, tilled, planted, watered or harvested, each forage, chop or catch, each load started at a station, each crate shipped and going into town takes one. Nothing on the farm moves until you end the day, whether the app is open, closed or left alone for a week. Ending the day runs the night: the bin pays, watered crops grow a stage, the stations' loads finish and the date turns. The morning opens on a card of what the night brought, and ten actions again.

The calendar and the day

Seasons, days and the year

Four seasons of 12 days make a 48-day year. Day 1 is Spring 1 of year 1, and day d falls in: - its year: ⌈d ÷ 48⌉; - its season: ⌊((d − 1) mod 48) ÷ 12⌋, counting Spring 0, Summer 1, Autumn 2 and Winter 3; - its day of the season: ((d − 1) mod 12) + 1.

The season sets: - what you can plant and which seeds the shop sells (the field); - what the woods' edge and the pond's edge bring home (Gathering); - which goods are in demand at the bin (Goods and gold); - what withers: on a season's last night, growing crops that don't grow in the next season wither.

Festivals

Each season's 10th day holds its festival, and Winter 12, the year's last day, also holds the Harvest Cup. Festivals are dates only: the calendar marks them, the morning says "Flower Show tomorrow" the day before and "Flower Show today" on the day, and the Festival ground on the map stays locked ("Opens soon"). No contest is played and no prize is given.

The story's festivals. The pack's world.json may rename, move or add festivals (the world in the pack); the year is then the story's everywhere: the calendar, the date chip, the morning, festival night, and festival triggers and gates. - One of the game's five keeps its game rule under the story's name, wherever it falls: its festival night, its news, the triggers and gates naming it, and the contest its rank challenge counts once the festivals are built. A moved one plays all of it on its new day every year, and none on its old day. - One of the story's own is a festival day and nothing more: marked, said, its night, and its triggers. - One festival a day: a move onto a day another holds is skipped, and that festival keeps the game's day.

A festival day also opens a festival night for the evening, and once the household's finale is reached, the Festival of Vows returns on its day each year. Both are the romance's.

A day: ten actions

  • The day holds 10 actions. The bar's actions chip counts what's left ("7/10"), every button that spends one says so ("Water · 1 action", "Fish · 1"), and a side panel says "7 actions left of 10". With none left, an action is refused ("No actions left today.") and its buttons are greyed.
  • One tap of work is one action: a tile cleared, tilled, planted, watered or harvested; a forage, a chop or a catch; a station's load started; a crate shipped; going into town. Looking is free, and so are building a place, the Dorm, talks, gifts, her events and the evening; the shop is free once you're in town.
  • Going into town takes one action and opens the town and its shop for the rest of the day.
  • The last action opens a card over whatever page it was spent on, once: "Out of actions · The day's work is done." with End the day and Not yet (to build or spend the evening first). The day never ends by itself.
  • Special days grant actions for events only, on top of the ten: New Year's, each festival, and the birthday of each Uma you've met. The save counts them apart (today.eventActions), and nothing shows or spends them yet: they wait for the festivals' and the town's events.
  • Nothing reads the device's clock. A load is ready the next morning and a watered tile grows at night, so reopening the app moves nothing.
  • Your changes are stamped with the real time, which syncing between devices compares; that stamp never moves the date.

End the day

End the day, the gold button at the bar's right end, ends the day.

The nudge. The button reads All set and glows once the day's actions are spent (a still glow under reduced motion); before that it reads End day. Its spoken name says what's left: "End the day: 4 actions left · 2 dry · 1 ripe".

Counts asWhen
Actions leftThe day's ten less those spent
DryA tile growing a crop, not ripe and not watered today: it won't grow tonight
RipeA ripe tile waiting to be harvested

The nudge reads the save as it stands, so every device shows the same.

The tap opens the End-the-day sheet over whatever page you're on: - All set, or the Menu's "Ask before ending the day" switched off: the day ends at once. - Otherwise one line says what's left, "4 actions left · 2 dry", with End anyway and Back.

Either way the morning opens on the farm map. The tap holds the day it was meant for: a second tap, or a save that another device has already moved on, ends nothing more. The night reads the game's master data (each Uma's talents and birthday) and the story pack (her favourite line, her trigger to meet, the ties), which the farm loads as it opens; if either can't load, the sheet says so with Try again.

"Ask before ending the day" is a switch in the Menu: on by default, kept on the device and never in the save.

Tonight. The tap first opens Tonight whenever an evening can be spent with someone or anyone is married: the evening's row, where you sleep once married, the nudge's line, and End the day / Back. Ending the day never waits on the evening. The night plays as a fade: the screen dims to dusk and the morning comes up from it (a plain cut with reduced motion). Tonight is the romance's.

The night, step by step

The night is one fixed run of steps over the save:

  1. The bin pays. Everything shipped today pays at its price × stars × demand on the day it was shipped, so a season's last night still pays that season's demand. Gold rises and the bin empties. Before it, once anyone is married, tonight's house is noted.
  2. Crops grow. Each growing tile watered today grows one stage, and one whose stages reach its days − 0.5 turns ripe; a dry tile doesn't grow. A farmer Uma's night chores each water a dry growing tile first. Then every tile dries for the next day.
  3. Animals. Nothing changes: no animals live on the farm.
  4. The stations' loads finish. Every load started today is done: its goods go into the Warehouse at their stars and the station is free for the morning. Side by side's and the perks' extras and foragers' and fishers' night chores go straight into the Warehouse too.
  5. Workshops. Nothing changes: no workshop is open.
  6. Ranks and bonds. Every Uma who worked today earns a day's rank points and ranks up where her challenge is met; bonds tick and her line pays. Nobody works yet (the farmer works alone, and the farmhands come later), so none of these move. Shared houses pay, spouses level, and well-fed days count down by one.
  7. The date turns. On a season's last night, growing crops that don't grow next season wither first. Then the date moves on, and the whole of today's record clears: the day's actions and event actions, going into town, the places worked, chats, gifts, praise, events, evenings and invitations.
  8. The farm's news. The places paid for yesterday stand; a Dorm level you bought takes effect and swaps arrive; the Diary pays its milestones and rows and the Farm Rank is recomputed; anyone in town whose trigger the farm now holds is met; the ring bench opens the night after the first Romance 8, and the household's milestones are noted.
  9. The morning. A wedding's reception is served, the house's morning given, spouses move into rooms, the Festival of Vows is held, praise, birthdays and anniversaries are noted, morning items are left and the morning's invitations drawn; then the morning summary is built from what changed.

A night without the story pack runs the farm's steps and skips the romance's: no meetings, no favourites, no bonds and no line pay. The farm waits for the pack before it ends a day, so in play every night has it.

Notifications

The farm sends no notifications. With days as turns nothing finishes while the app is closed, so Account's Notifications card reads "Off: days are turns".

Growing

  • A watered tile grows one stage a night (night step 2), and turns ripe once its stages reach its days − 0.5: a 4-day Carrot is ripe after four watered nights, a 10-day Asparagus after ten. A dry tile doesn't grow, and loses nothing.
  • Every tile dries overnight, so a crop takes one Water a day it grows: a Carrot costs a plant, four waters and a harvest, six actions for its one crop.
  • Ripe crops wait as long as you like, and are never watered.
  • A farmer Uma's night chores water a dry growing tile each (see the romance in the field).

Harvesting

  • Each crop draws its own stars. A tile gives its yield in crops, and each draws its stars from the harvest's quality points: the crop's level (+0.1 a level above 1) and the Hoe (+0.25 a tier above Basic). At crop level 1 that's always ★1. Every farm has the Basic Hoe, and nothing on the farm upgrades it yet.
  • Seeds back: each harvest may drop one own seed of its crop: 5%, and +5% a Hoe tier above Basic.
  • Crop points: each harvested tile adds 1 point to its crop, or 2 if it was planted from own seeds.
  • The tile stays tilled and empty, ready to plant again.

Regrowing

Mint, Tomato, Basil and Bell Pepper regrow in 3 watered nights, Eggplant and Corn in 4. After a harvest the tile keeps its crop and starts again at stage 0, so it needs watering again. It keeps its seed's kind, own or the shop's, for its crop points. Out of its season a regrowing crop doesn't start again: a ripe Tomato harvested on Autumn 1 leaves the tile empty.

Withering

On a season's last night, after the night's growth and before the date turns, every crop still growing (a regrowing one too) that doesn't grow in the next season withers, and its tile is left tilled and empty. Ripe crops never wither: they wait to be harvested. Only Corn (Summer into Autumn) and Cabbage (Winter into Spring) carry over. Onion grows in Spring and Autumn, which don't meet, so a growing Onion withers at the end of both.

Catch sizes and records

Each fish is measured as it lands:

  • Size = min + (max − min) × u^(8 ÷ k), in cm to a tenth.
  • k = 1 + 0.25 × the fisher's rank (Novice 0 to Legend 5) + 0.1 × the talent's step (G 0 to S 7) + 0.05 × the Rod's tier: the farmer's is the table's above.

One price rule

Every good's price at ★1 comes from one rule: 1. The ladder: a family's grade G sells at the ladder's price × the family's factor, rounded to 5g. 2. Refined goods (a station's output) sell at ×1.5 their input's price, rounded to 5g. 3. Made goods sell at ×1.25 the sum of their inputs at ★1, rounded to 5g. The rings are priced this way (a Copper Ring is 2 Copper Ingots and a Cut Quartz: 165g × 1.25 = 205g); dishes have no price. 4. Crops have their own prices wherever they come from, so the seven crops that are also forest goods keep them. A crop's refined form is ×1.5. 5. Stars add +25% a star, never compounded: ★1 ×1, ★2 ×1.25, ★3 ×1.5, ★4 ×1.75, ★5 ×2. 6. Demand adds +20% to the season's families, on top of the stars.

The shipping bin

The bin takes anything with a price and pays at night. There's no cap on selling, and nothing is ever bought back.

  • Ship a crate · 1 action: pick what to ship in the Warehouse's pick mode, as many goods and stars as you like, and one action puts the whole crate in today's bin. Shipping again the same day is another crate and another action. Seeds and dishes don't sell ("That doesn't sell").
  • Ship all · 1 action: a good's sheet in the Warehouse ships every star of it as one crate.
  • Take back: a whole line from the bin back to the Warehouse, free, any time until the night. After the night, a sale is final.
  • The estimate: "About 1,240g tonight", at today's prices.
  • The bin's sheet (from the bin on the map, the gold chip or a morning line): "About 1,240g tonight", today's lines (icon, "Carrot ★2 × 3", the line's gold, In demand), each with Take back, and Ship · 1 action, which opens the Warehouse to pick a crate. Empty, it says "Empty" and "Tap Ship to pick goods. They pay tonight." Its help (i) says the rule in three lines.

At night (step 1): - each good and star pays price × stars × demand on the day it was shipped, rounded to 5g a unit, × the count; - gold rises by the total; - the farm's gold shipped rises by the same (the Farm Rank's points, 1 per 100g, and its shipping milestones); - each good's count joins the Diary's shipped goods; - the bin empties.

The morning's bin section lists each good and star sold, with its gold and In demand, then the total. A line's gold is the night's own and is never priced again.

Part 2 — The Umas and the farm's progress

Every character lives in the town, where a different few are out each day for a talk, and you meet her there when her trigger is met; the welcome list comes to the Gate, one a morning, from day 1. Once met, she moves into the Dorm while a bed is free, else she lives in town until you invite her. The Dorm's beds cap who lives and works on the farm at once. Every Uma has a talent, G to S, in each of nine activities, read from her stats, and climbs six ranks in each by working and meeting a challenge. The farm climbs a ladder of its own, the Farm Rank, from what it earns and grows, and grows by building: the rank offers each place, and you build it with gold and the farm's goods. There are no global chapters and no farm-wide story.

The roster

The roster is everyone you've met, and her joined the day you met her. Who is home (on the farm) is capped by the Dorm's beds; everyone else on the roster lives in town as a friend. A spouse in her house is on the farm too, without a Dorm bed. Only Umas on the farm play their events and evenings (and will work, once the farmhands come); chats and gifts reach everyone you've met, on the farm or in town.

How Umas join

You meet her in town, when her trigger is met, or at the Gate, the welcome list's, one a morning: see meeting her. Met, she joins the roster at Friendship 0: home while a bed is free, else she lives in town, and Invite to the farm on her profile brings her into a free bed at once. There's no cap on how many you meet. Nothing is drawn.

Living in town, and swaps

Everyone on the roster past the beds lives in town. She keeps her ranks and her route, and earns nothing: no rank points, no events or evenings. Chats and gifts still reach her, and she's out in town on her days like anyone (see the town).

  • A swap sends one Uma home to town and brings one from town home in her place. It is free and unlimited, and it happens the next morning: the one leaving goes to town and the one arriving takes her bed.
  • Who can go to town: any Uma home. Refused: one already leaving ("She leaves tomorrow.") and anyone not home ("She isn't on the farm.").
  • Who can come home: anyone on the roster who lives in town and isn't already arriving ("She arrives tomorrow."). One home already is refused ("She's on the farm already."), and so is a spouse in her house ("She lives in her house.").
  • Leaving tomorrow: until the night, the swap picker shows an Uma a swap takes out greyed out with "Leaves tomorrow".
  • Calling off: a swap can be called off any time before the night; she stays.

The game holds the cast, the ties, each bible by character id, the event ids each route scripts, the bond levels each tie's pair has a conversation at, the household, the opening, the town and the world. Routes and pairs' conversations load on demand, the first time they're needed.

How the app gets the pack

  • The release reads the private pack with the game's own reader and releases any pack, partial or not: it lists what it skipped (the errors) and the notes, and refuses only when there's no pack folder. It holds the pack as the game reads it, every broken part already skipped and every other field kept, and always a cast and a ties file (empty when the pack has none). It writes each file minified, named with the first 8 hex of its SHA-256 (story/<name>.<hash8>.json), and an index, story.json: every file with its path, size and hash, the event ids each route scripts, and the bond levels each pair holds. The same pack gives the same release.
  • The ship (pnpm story:ship, run where the story is written) checks the pack, builds the release, publishes it and backs the drafts up. Every ship is numbered (one more than the release the bucket held, or 1), dated, and has a note saying what's new (one line, 160 characters at most). The publish uploads the hash-named files first and the index last, to a private bucket. Old files stay, so a phone holding an older index still reads it, and a rollback publishes an earlier index again under a new number, its note "Rolled back to n". A pack the bucket already plays isn't published again.
  • farm-api serves it to the owner only: the index fresh on every read, the hash-named files cached for good, and nothing else (404).
  • On the phone, the boot reads the index, then the cast, the ties, the bibles, the household, the opening, the town and the world; each route and pair loads when it's first needed. Every file is checked against the index's hash and kept in the content cache, so a release's file downloads once. The boot and the night wait for the pack, as they wait for master data.
  • The last good index stays on the phone. When the index can't be read (offline, or the server failing), the boot plays the last version kept. The phone reads each file with the same reader, so a broken part is skipped there too. A release that can't load (no index and none kept, a file whose hash doesn't match, a route or pair that isn't what the index lists) stops the boot with Try again.
  • No pack published plays no pack: when farm-api serves none at all (a 404 for the index), the game plays with no pack, on the default story: master data's first five are the cast, so a fresh farm opens and plays whole. The boot waits for master data as well as the index, since the default story reads it.
  • "New story arrived." A fresh index whose number isn't the one the phone kept last is a new release. Once its pack has loaded, the phone keeps its number (farm.story-news, a few bytes) until the next night, which opens the morning's New today with "New story arrived · its note", tagged New; a tap opens the People page. It shows once a release on each phone; a release that arrives before the night replaces the one waiting. The save keeps the line's number only, never the note: the card reads the note from the release the game plays, and says "New story arrived" alone for another one. A new farm starts with none waiting, since its opening plays the story. An offline boot on the kept index, and a release published unnumbered (story:publish), say nothing.
  • Account's Story row shows the release the game plays: "Release 4, 14 Nov 2026" (its number and the day it shipped; an unnumbered one by its id and build date), or "No story yet" when none plays.

The words under the field say why ("Enter a name", "Letters, spaces, ' and - only", "16 characters at most"). Entering it is the farm's first player change. Part 4 — The save

The save, as a player meets it

One save, on the phone first

  • One save per player: the signed-in Google account's farm, one copy on the phone and one on the server.
  • Every change is saved on the phone at once. Play never waits on the network once the phone holds a save.
  • A sync follows 1.5 seconds after the last change, when the app comes back to the foreground, when the network returns, and as the app opens.
  • Opening the app: splash, then Google sign-in, then the farm. The app opens the phone's save for this account; with none, the server's; with none there either, a new farm on day 1. A save from before v5 stops at the start-over card first (see Save versions).
  • The app waits for the story pack as it opens; if the pack can't load, the app stops and offers Try again.
  • A new phone needs the server once. Offline with no save on the phone, the app stops with "The game couldn't open on this phone." and Try again, since a new farm made offline could later outrank the real one.
  • Only a player's own change moves the save's "last changed" time. Starting the new farm over an older save and syncing never do.

  • A change made during a sync starts another sync right after.

The conflict rule. When the phone and the server both changed, the save changed last wins whole; a tie keeps this phone's. If the server's wins, this phone's unsynced save is kept first as a safety copy ("Replaced by a newer save"), and every page says "A newer save replaced this device's changes. A refused upload. The server stores what the app sends and never edits a save. It refuses: - a save it can't read (a bad shape, unknown keys, or a version other than v5: an old build's older save); - a save belonging to another account; - a save of an older version than the one it holds.

Each refused upload is kept on the server as a rescue copy (the newest one), so play that couldn't be uploaded isn't lost; nothing restores it automatically. The phone keeps its changes, and Account says "Sync paused".

  • Nothing goes silently: a phone save with unsynced play is kept first as a "Before the new farm" safety copy (if it can't be kept, nothing changes and the card says so, with the button to try again). The new farm syncs up as it is, and the first time the server stores a v5 over the older save it copies the older one, whole, to a backup (saveBackups/{uid}-v4 for a v4). The server never writes into a save.
  • An older save met later (the server's in a sync, or in a conflict) becomes the same new farm and uploads.

A newer save on an older build

A save newer than v5 comes only from a newer build. When this one meets it: - As it opens (the phone's save, or on a new phone the server's): the opening screen says "This save is from a newer version of the farm." with Try again, and the app asks for the new build at once, so the update prompt comes up; Restart brings a build that can play it. - On the server, at a sync: the sync stops before any upload ("Sync paused"), so nothing writes over the newer save. - An upload of an older version the server receives anyway is refused and kept as a rescue copy.

A save the app can't read at all on the phone is kept as a "Couldn't be read" safety copy, and the app opens the server's save instead.
