What this game is

Uma Farm is the owner's private, landscape Home Screen game. Start by tending the farm and meeting the Umas in town. Invite friends to join the crew. As they take over daily work, the farmer has more time for town, outings and married life. Farms keep progressing after the closing celebration. Save v6 grows in place, with device safety copies and cloud sync.

The finish line

The core progression is playable through the Restaurant, contests, expeditions, marriage and the 50-spouse celebration. Remaining release work is to confirm the installed iPhone's performance, finish farm-work feedback, and exercise recovery and deployment checks. Open checks

The phases

Phase 0: Clean slate (done)

Established one save, one private deployment and the mechanics reference. Git retains the earlier specs and finished packets.

Phase 1: One game, built around the Umas (done)

Shipped the landscape loop, building progression, town introductions, individual routes, default story and the shared dialogue engine.

Phase 2: The whole farm

The ranch, mines, sea, expeditions, Restaurant, contests, Library, Forge and long-game milestones are in. Current mechanics describe their rules. The early scaling studies record the rules and results at the time. Four-season crew balance covers the later automatic work scheduler.

How we work from here

  • Update game mechanics with rule changes and regenerate docs with pnpm docs:update.
  • Keep the roadmap for planned work and the handoff for active checks. Git keeps finished packets.
  • Follow the standing story guide and art guide. Publish their private content separately from app code.
