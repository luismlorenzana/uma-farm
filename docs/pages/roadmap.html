The road to a full game

Start here. This page says what the farm is, where it stands, what "a full game" means for it, and the phases that get it there. How the game works today is in game-mechanics.md, the farm's progression in the farm's progress. How it's built is in technical/architecture.md, and the cloud in technical/infrastructure.md. The page view is pages/roadmap.html.

What this game is

  • Personal, never public. It's the owner's own game, on the owner's iPhone, reachable only with the owner's Google account. Nothing here plans for other players, stores or distribution.
  • Still being built. The deployed site is the work in progress, not something to preserve: saves can start over, and rules can change, whenever the game needs it.
  • Built whole, played live. Every system ships and plays from day one, with plain placeholders wherever the story hasn't reached yet. The story arrives over time in packs, and each pack unlocks more of what the game already has. No pack has to be complete.
  • The story is free. The story session builds the world, the backgrounds and the characters however it wants. Much of it may never appear in the game; it informs the dialogue that does. The game asks the story for nothing it can't play without.
  • No global chapters, no farm-wide story. Each Uma's story is hers, in her own chapters, from the day you meet her to married life.
  • The farm grows by what you earn and grow. The Farm Rank, from income and farmed goods, says what you may build, and you build it with gold and goods. The homestead and the town grow with your life with the Umas: the n-th wedding opens house n.
  • A lean start, then the town. You name your farmer, read a few lines, and you're on the farm. Every character lives in town. You choose who to talk to, and a character's story begins when her trigger is met. The story's welcome list (five by default) comes to you in the first days.

The finish line

The farm is a full game when all eight of these hold:

  1. One build, one look. No build flags, no preview switch. The HD-2D farm is Home, and the 2D Hub is retired.
  2. Everything live, filled by the story over time. Every system plays with placeholders, and each story pack adds what it has.
  3. The town of everyone. Every character lives in town. You choose who to talk to, and her story begins when her trigger is met.
  4. Each Uma's story to its end, and the farm built out. Her chapters play from meeting her to married life. Every place can be built, and the homestead fills with houses. The game keeps going: years, festivals, the household, records.
  5. Every place works. The ranch, the Mine and the Quarry, the Kitchen with its cooking game, expeditions, and the Restaurant's and the Forge's games.
  6. Real art and sound everywhere. No cast standees, no stand-in frames, farm-work animations, music and sound effects.
  7. It feels finished. A quick start, places that explain themselves the first time, settings (sound, motion, text size), every control labelled and easy to tap, and pacing proven by simulation and by your play.
  8. It's safe and steady. A save that never loses progress, error reports that reach us, a full playthrough tested on every push, and a check on your phone before each release.

Phase 1 runs as two tracks, one piece at a time each: the game's frame, and the HD-2D farm. Phase 3 starts once a workshop stands on the stage. Phase 4's error reports and save safety are small and can come any time.

Phase 0: Clean slate (done)

  • The decision log, the finished task packets, the old specs and their pages are deleted. Git keeps them: commit 70071ac is the last that has them all.
  • One document says what the game is (game-mechanics.md), with its tables generated from the code. This roadmap says where it's going.
  • Code and files with no purpose are removed, and comments no longer point at deleted documents.

Later: parcels to the other games

The farm sends items to uma-quest, uma-rpg and uma-racer, one way. It's parked until the farm's own game is done.

How we work from here

  • game-mechanics.md is what the game is. Every rule change updates it in the same commit, and its tables regenerate from the code (pnpm docs:update).
  • This roadmap is where the game is going. A finished step leaves it, and git keeps the history.
  • Packets are for the game's work only. A step gets a packet in handoff/tasks/ when it's next, and the packet is deleted when it ships. handoff/README.md lists what's open now. The story has no packet of requirements: F40 only says how its work reaches the game.
  • There's no decision log. A commit says what changed and why. Your calls go into the roadmap or the mechanics document they change.

Your steps now

  • The farm starts over on your phone: open uma-farm.web.app, see the start-over card, start the new farm, and play a day.
  • Her chapters on your phone: open an Uma's profile and see her four chapters, the one she's in, and what opens the next; the morning after her confession says "A new chapter with ‹name›".
  • Say go on the workshops (Phase 2), which her chapters unblock.
  • Meeting her on your phone: say hello at the Gate each morning, and meet someone in town on her third day.
  • The town on your phone: open the Town at the foot of the farm map, talk to a few, find someone in People.
  • Keep writing the story however you like.
  • The HD-2D farm on your phone, when you have a minute.
